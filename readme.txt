This is a web minesweeper game.
It is for school assignment and pratices.

repo:
jammingg/VFS-T1-HTML-minesweeper

Open a local server to Play
command: 
npx http-server -c -1

Player control:
Left click to reveal and chording (the both mouse click clicked featrue).
Right click to mark.
Press the smiley face at the top conner to restart.
